Terms & Conditions

Last Updated: August 2026 • The Slow House - Cafe, Shirur Park, Hubballi

1. Cafe House Rules & Ambience

The Slow House is dedicated to slow living, peaceful work, reading, and gentle social gatherings. We kindly ask all patrons to maintain a respectful, welcoming volume, especially during acoustic sessions and study hours.

2. Table Reservations

  • Reserved tables are held for a maximum grace period of 15 minutes past your selected time slot before being released to walk-in guests.
  • For weekend acoustic nights, seat allocations are prioritized based on advance reservation confirmations.
  • If you need to reschedule or cancel, please inform us via WhatsApp at least 1 hour prior to your booking.

3. Food & Beverage Orders

  • All specialty coffees, artisanal pastries, and savory items are prepared fresh to order. Preparation times may vary during peak evening hours (5:00 PM – 9:00 PM).
  • Please notify our baristas in advance regarding severe dietary restrictions, lactose intolerance (oat/almond milk options available), or nut allergies.

4. Acoustic Sessions & Events

  • Artists performing at The Slow House must adhere to scheduled time slots and sound limit guidelines.
  • Audience members agree to maintain a supportive, quiet listening environment during acoustic and poetry performances.
